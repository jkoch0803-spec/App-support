EngiCalc Pro — Privacy policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

EngiCalc Pro collects no data. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

What we collect

Nothing. EngiCalc Pro does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data or usage data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising and no tracking of any kind, in the Free edition or the Full Toolkit.

What stays on your device

Your settings (units, precision, appearance), the values you last typed into each calculator, your favorites and your recent list are saved only on your device. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The Full Toolkit is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. The app asks Apple only whether your Apple ID owns the Full Toolkit, and remembers the answer on your device. We never see your name, Apple ID, payment details or receipts. Apple's handling of the purchase is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Sharing

If you share a result, the app hands the text to your device's share sheet, and you choose where it goes. The app itself sends nothing.

Network

Calculations run entirely on your device and work offline. The only network traffic is between your device and Apple's App Store when you load the Full Toolkit price, buy it or restore it.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on this page, with a new date. Questions can be sent through the support link on the app's App Store page.
