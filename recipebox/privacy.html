Mama's Recipe Box — Privacy policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Mama's Recipe Box collects no data. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

What we collect

Nothing. Mama's Recipe Box does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data or usage data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising and no tracking of any kind, in the Free edition or Pro.

What stays on your device

Your recipes, photos of recipe cards and dishes, holiday menus, shopping lists, collections and settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Photos and text recognition

If you add a photo, it stays in the app's own storage on your device. "Read text from photo" uses on-device text recognition (Apple Vision on iPhone). The photo is not uploaded for OCR.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. The app asks Apple only whether your Apple ID owns Pro, and remembers the answer on your device. We never see your name, Apple ID, payment details or receipts. Apple's handling of the purchase is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Sharing

If you share a recipe, cookbook PDF or .mamasrecipe file, the app hands it to your device's share sheet, Messages or Mail, and you choose where it goes. The app itself sends nothing.

Network

Recipes and OCR work offline. The only network traffic is between your device and Apple's App Store when you load the Pro price, buy it or restore it.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on this page, with a new date. Questions can be sent through the support link on the app's App Store page.
